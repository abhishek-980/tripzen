1. Overview & Commitment to Privacy

TripZen Travel Technologies (“TripZen”, “We”, “Our”, Govt. of India MSME: UDYAM-UP-28-0250895) respects the privacy of every traveler who uses our platform. This Privacy Policy details our practices regarding personal data collection, processing, cloud storage, and user rights in compliance with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDPA) and the Information Technology (Reasonable Security Practices and Procedures and Sensitive Personal Data or Information) Rules, 2011.

2. What Information We Collect

We only collect information necessary to deliver verified adventure tour reservations and secure expedition booking:

  • Account Identifiers: Full Name, Email Address, Mobile Phone Number, Age, Gender, City, and Profile Avatar.
  • Google OAuth Data: When signing in with Google, we receive your Google ID, verified email, and profile photo via Supabase Auth.
  • Travel & Trail Preferences: Intended Himalayan summits, desired trekking style, departure dates, fitness rating, and past trekking background.
  • Booking Records: Transaction identifiers, selected package, booking status, and dates. Note: Payment card details and UPI PINs are processed directly by RBI-licensed payment aggregators and are never seen or stored by TripZen.
  • Encrypted Communications: User-to-user messages exchanged in travel inboxes are securely persisted with end-to-end access restricted solely to the two chatting travelers.

🔒 Zero-Spam & Zero-Selling Guarantee

TripZen will NEVER sell, rent, or trade your personal information or phone number to marketing agencies, loan aggregators, or third-party advertisers. Your information is used exclusively to facilitate your mountain journey and companion matching.

3. How We Use Your Data

  • Companion Compatibility Matching: Aligning you with compatible trekkers heading to the same trail during your departure dates.
  • Booking Confirmations: Dispatching instant booking confirmations, vouchers, and expedition manifests via our verified WhatsApp Cloud API and Email.
  • On-Ground Mountain Safety: Providing local licensed expedition leaders with verified trekker manifests for forest permits, emergency rescue logistics, and campsite headcounts.
  • Account Recovery: Facilitating secure password resets and multi-factor session validation.

4. Data Storage & Security Safeguards

We employ industry-leading technical and organizational security controls:

  • Encryption in Transit: 256-bit TLS 1.3 / SSL encryption across all API requests and socket connections.
  • Cloud Database Security: Databases hosted on Supabase Cloud PostgreSQL with Row Level Security (RLS) policies and encrypted at rest.
  • Automated Backups: Redundant cloud snapshot persistence ensures your bookings and conversation histories survive infrastructure redeploys without data loss.

5. Sharing with Authorized Service Partners

We share limited information strictly on a need-to-know basis with trusted infrastructure partners:

  • Supabase Auth & Database: For secure authentication and encrypted cloud data persistence.
  • WhatsApp Cloud API: For automated real-time booking confirmation and emergency trek notifications.
  • RBI-Authorized Payment Gateways: For processing UPI, Netbanking, and Card transactions securely.
  • Certified Expedition Operators: Providing trek leaders with traveler manifests for forest permits and altitude medical clearance.

6. Your Privacy Rights

Under Indian privacy regulations, you retain full control over your personal data:

  • Access & Correction: Review and update your profile, photo, or travel preferences anytime directly inside the app.
  • Data Portability: Request an export of your account and booking records.
  • Right to Erasure (Account Deletion): Request permanent deletion of your account, chats, and traveler profile by emailing our Grievance Officer.
