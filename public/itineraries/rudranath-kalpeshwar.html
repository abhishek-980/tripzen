Trek Overview: Rudranath is the Fourth Kedar, where the serene face (mukh) of Lord Shiva is worshipped in a natural stone cave. Regarded as the most scenic trek in Uttarakhand, the trail traverses vast rolling emerald meadows (Panar & Pitradhar Bugyal) with dramatic, close-up panoramas of Mount Nanda Devi, Trishul, Nanda Ghunti, and Chaukhamba. The itinerary also covers Kalpeshwar (Fifth Kedar), where Lord Shiva’s matted hair (jata) is worshipped.

📅 Detailed Day-by-Day Itinerary

DAY 1 Rishikesh to Sagar Village Base
🏔️ 6,600 ft • 🚶 Drive 215 km (8 hours)

Drive along the Alaknanda valley via Devprayag and Karnaprayag to Sagar Village near Gopeshwar. Orientation walk, briefing session, and hot Garhwali dinner.

🍽️ Meals: Lunch on route & Dinner in Sagar
⛺ Stay: Homestay in Sagar Village
DAY 2 Sagar Village to Panar Bugyal via Lyti Bugyal
🏔️ 10,800 ft • 🚶 12 km steep trek (6-7 hours)

Ascend through dense oak, pine, and rhododendron forests to Pun Bugyal and Lyti Bugyal. Climb the switchbacks to reach the breathtaking high-altitude meadows of Panar Bugyal. Witness Trishul and Nanda Devi glowing in the sunset.

🍽️ Meals: Breakfast, Packed Lunch, Hot Soup & Camp Dinner
⛺ Stay: Alpine Dome Tents at Panar Bugyal
DAY 3 Panar Bugyal across Pitradhar to Rudranath Temple
🏔️ 11,800 ft • 🚶 8 km trek (4-5 hours)

Trek through endless ridge meadows to Pitradhar (12,500 ft). Offer prayers and descend into the mystical Rudranath valley. Attend the deeply moving evening aarti of Lord Shiva’s serene face (Nilkanth Mahadev) inside the natural rock sanctum.

🍽️ Meals: Breakfast, Hot Lunch, Evening Tea & Prasad Dinner
⛺ Stay: Temple Guesthouse / Tents at Rudranath
DAY 4 Rudranath to Helang / Urgam Valley & Kalpeshwar Ji Darshan
🏔️ 7,200 ft • 🚶 14 km descent + 20 km drive

Attend morning Rudrabhishek. Descend through the forested Dumak / Urgam trail. Reach Urgam Valley and walk to the ancient rock cave of Kalpeshwar Temple (5th Kedar) surrounded by Kalpvriksha wish-fulfilling tree.

🍽️ Meals: Breakfast, Trail Lunch & Dinner
⛺ Stay: Homestay in Urgam Valley
DAY 5 Urgam Valley to Rishikesh / Delhi Departure
🏔️ 1,120 ft • 🚶 Drive 230 km

Drive back along the sacred Alaknanda river to Rishikesh. Arrive in Rishikesh by 5:00 PM with completed Panch Kedar blessings.

🍽️ Meals: Breakfast & En-route lunch
⛺ Stay: Return Transit
