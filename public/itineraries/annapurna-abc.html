Annapurna Base Camp (ABC) Sanctuary Expedition

Surrounded by 8,000m Giants & Machapuchare (13,550 ft)

Region
Annapurna Conservation Area, Nepal
Max Altitude
13,550 ft / 4,130 m
Trek Distance
67 km total
Base Location
Pokhara / Nayapul
Stay Type
Traditional Sherpa Tea House Lodges
Meals
All 3 Wholesome Himalayan Meals (Dal Bhat Power)
Trek Overview: Annapurna Base Camp (ABC) is one of the most legendary treks in the world. Leading deep into the heart of the Annapurna Sanctuary, it surrounds you in an amphitheater of colossal peaks including Annapurna I (8,091m - 10th highest in the world), Annapurna South, Hiunchuli, and the sacred unclimbed Fishtail (Machapuchare).

📅 Detailed Day-by-Day Itinerary

DAY 1 Kathmandu to Pokhara Scenic Transit
🏔️ 2,700 ft • 🚶 Scenic tourist coach / flight

Arrive in Pokhara, the lakeside adventure capital of Nepal. Walk along Phewa Lake, check equipment, meet Sherpa guides, and prepare TIMS & ACAP permits.

🍽️ Meals: Dinner in Pokhara
⛺ Stay: Hotel in Pokhara
DAY 2 Pokhara to Nayapul Drive & Trek to Ghandruk / Ulleri
🏔️ 6,360 ft • 🚶 Drive + 5-6 hours trek

Drive to Nayapul and start trek through charming Gurung villages and terraced rice paddies to Ghandruk / Ulleri with views of Machapuchare.

🍽️ Meals: Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner
⛺ Stay: Tea House Lodge
DAY 3 Trek to Chhomrong (Gateway to the Sanctuary)
🏔️ 7,120 ft • 🚶 5-6 hours trek

Hike through oak and rhododendron forests, descending to the Chhomrong Khola and ascending to the magnificent amphitheater village of Chhomrong.

🍽️ Meals: Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner
⛺ Stay: Tea House Lodge in Chhomrong
DAY 4 Chhomrong to Bamboo / Dovan
🏔️ 8,200 ft • 🚶 5-6 hours trek

Trail drops through stone steps into a bamboo and fern canyon alongside the Modi Khola river.

🍽️ Meals: Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner
⛺ Stay: Tea House Lodge in Dovan
DAY 5 Dovan to Deurali & Machapuchare Base Camp (MBC)
🏔️ 12,139 ft • 🚶 6 hours trek

Ascend past Hinku cave into the alpine valley between Machapuchare and Hiunchuli to arrive at MBC (12,139 ft).

🍽️ Meals: Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner
⛺ Stay: Tea House Lodge at MBC
DAY 6 MBC to Annapurna Base Camp (13,550 ft) Sunrise Push
🏔️ 13,550 ft • 🚶 3 hours trek

Trek into the sanctuary basin to reach ABC at 13,550 ft. Marvel at the 360-degree wall of Annapurna I, Annapurna South, Tent Peak, and Gangapurna.

🍽️ Meals: Breakfast, Lunch, Hot Camp Dinner
⛺ Stay: Tea House Lodge at ABC
DAY 7 ABC Sunrise over Annapurna Massif & Trek down to Bamboo
🏔️ 7,500 ft • 🚶 6-7 hours descent

Witness golden sunrise over Annapurna I. Descend back through MBC and Deurali to Bamboo.

🍽️ Meals: Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner
⛺ Stay: Tea House Lodge in Bamboo
DAY 8 Bamboo to Jhinu Danda & Natural Hot Springs
🏔️ 5,800 ft • 🚶 5 hours trek

Trek to Jhinu Danda. Relax and soak tired muscles in the natural thermal hot springs beside the Modi Khola river.

🍽️ Meals: Breakfast, Lunch, Celebration Dinner
⛺ Stay: Tea House Lodge in Jhinu Danda
DAY 9 Jhinu Danda to Siwai & Drive to Pokhara
🏔️ 2,700 ft • 🚶 3 hours walk + 2 hours drive

Walk across the iconic long suspension bridges to Siwai. Drive back to Pokhara for celebratory dinner.

🍽️ Meals: Breakfast, Lunch & Dinner
⛺ Stay: Hotel in Pokhara
DAY 10 Pokhara to Kathmandu Return Transit
🏔️ 4,600 ft • 🚶 Transit to Kathmandu

Scenic return coach to Kathmandu. Expedition concludes with incredible memories!

🍽️ Meals: Breakfast
⛺ Stay: Trip concludes in Kathmandu
