Shipping & Delivery Policy

Information on how travel confirmations, digital vouchers, and on-ground expedition services are fulfilled.

1. Nature of Services Provided

TripZen Travel Technologies is an experiential travel platform offering digital companion matchmaking, curated trekking expeditions, and mountain travel packages. We do not sell or ship physical merchandise or physical goods. Consequently, traditional postal or courier shipping does not apply to our platform.

2. Digital Delivery Timelines & Process

All bookings, tickets, and vouchers are delivered digitally through automated electronic channels:

  • Instant Digital Confirmation: Within 15 minutes of successful payment completion, your official Booking Reference ID (e.g. TZ-XXXXXX) and detailed digital itinerary receipt are delivered via WhatsApp (to your registered mobile number) and Email.
  • In-App Dashboard Access: Your booking status and details are accessible 24/7 immediately in your My Bookings section on the TripZen dashboard.
  • Pre-Departure Briefing & Leader Contact: Exactly 48 hours before the scheduled trek start date, you will receive your final Pre-Trek Dispatch via WhatsApp and Email. This includes:
    • Exact Base Camp Reporting Location (e.g., Manali Private Bus Stand, Rishikesh Natraj Chowk, Leh Main Market).
    • Designated Reporting Time and Trek Leader / Mountain Guide phone number.
    • Local emergency contact details and mandatory mountain gear checklist.

📲 Zero Shipping Fees

Because all booking confirmations, vouchers, and emergency travel manifests are delivered digitally, there are zero shipping, handling, or courier charges levied on any transaction.

3. Fulfillment of On-Ground Travel Services

The physical fulfillment of your expedition begins at the designated reporting base camp on the start date selected during checkout. Licensed local mountain guides and certified trek leads will verify your digital voucher on your phone (no physical paper printout required).

4. Delay in Receiving Digital Confirmations

In rare cases of network delays or incorrect phone/email entries during checkout, if you do not receive your digital confirmation within 30 minutes of payment:

  • Check your spam/junk folder for confirmation emails.
  • Contact our immediate support desk via WhatsApp at +91 89206 32874 or email Support.tripzen@gmail.com. Our support team will manually re-dispatch your booking voucher within 1 business hour.
