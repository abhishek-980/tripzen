1. Who We Are

TripZen Travel Technologies is an experiential adventure travel and guided expedition platform founded by Abhishek Sharma. We specialize in organizing curated high-altitude Himalayan mountain treks, wilderness expeditions, and small-group adventure departures across Himachal Pradesh, Uttarakhand, Ladakh, and Jammu & Kashmir.

Every trek in our catalog is planned in direct partnership with certified local guides and licensed expedition operators. We believe in high mountain safety standards, eco-friendly leave-no-trace camping, and transparent all-inclusive pricing with zero hidden charges.

3. Our Safety & Expedition Standards

  • Wilderness First Responder (WFR) Certified Guides: All mountain departures are accompanied by licensed trek leads trained in high-altitude medicine, AMS management, and emergency evacuations.
  • Sanitized Alpine Swiss Tents: We provide triple-layer insulated sleeping bags, clean sleeping mats, and weather-tested alpine tents.
  • Forest Permits & Eco-Passes: All government forest entry fees, trail permits, and ecological conservation levies are procured before arrival.
  • Nutritious Trail Meals: Fresh, warm, vegetarian nutrition designed specifically for stamina in low-oxygen environments.
