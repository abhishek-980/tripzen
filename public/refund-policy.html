1. Overview

At TripZen Travel Technologies (“TripZen”, Govt. of India MSME: UDYAM-UP-28-0250895), we strive to keep our cancellation and refund process as flexible and transparent as possible. When you book a trek or expedition package, campsite reservations, guide allocations, forest department entry permits, and local safety equipment are procured in advance.

2. Standard Cancellation & Refund Matrix

If you choose to cancel a confirmed expedition booking, your refund is determined by the number of days prior to the trek departure date:

Cancellation Timeline Cash Refund to Bank TripZen Credit Voucher Option
15 days or more before departure 90% Refund (10% standard admin fee) 100% Voucher (Valid for 12 months)
7 to 14 days before departure 50% Refund 60% Voucher (Valid for 12 months)
Less than 7 days before departure No cash refund (Permits & logistics non-refundable) Reschedule Voucher (Transfer to upcoming batch for 20% logistics fee)

💡 TripZen 100% Credit Voucher Benefit

If you choose a TripZen Credit Voucher instead of a bank refund, you get 100% of your booking amount saved. The voucher can be redeemed against any trek or expedition across Himachal, Uttarakhand, Ladakh, or Kashmir within 365 days.

3. Natural Calamities & Force Majeure Trail Closures

Mountain environments can be unpredictable. If a trek is officially cancelled by TripZen or local authorities due to:

  • Severe weather warnings (blizzards, excessive snowfall, cloudbursts).
  • Landslides, flash floods, or road blockages en route to base camp.
  • Government or Forest Department trail closures.

Trekkers will be issued a 100% Free Rescheduling Voucher with zero deductions, valid for any available batch within 12 months.

4. Medical Emergencies Before Departure

If you are unable to participate due to a sudden acute medical illness or injury, submit a formal medical certificate issued by a registered medical practitioner at least 48 hours prior to trek departure to receive an 80% Travel Credit Voucher.

5. Refund Turnaround Time & Processing Method

  • Mode of Refund: All approved refunds are credited directly back to the original source of payment (same Bank Account, UPI ID, or Credit/Debit Card used during checkout).
  • Processing Timeline: Refunds are initiated within 24 to 48 hours of approval. Depending on your bank's clearance cycle, the funds will reflect in your account within 5 to 7 business days.
  • Zero Hidden Deductions: TripZen does not charge hidden dispute or convenience penalties.

6. How to Request a Cancellation or Refund

To cancel a booking and initiate a refund, follow these quick steps:

  1. Email our support desk at Support.tripzen@gmail.com or message our verified WhatsApp helpline at +91 89206 32874.
  2. Provide your Booking Reference ID (e.g. TZ-955612), registered phone number, and reason for cancellation.
  3. Our team will verify the timeline and respond within 24 hours with your refund transaction receipt or credit voucher code.
